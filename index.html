Your room, your screen

A personal TV behind the glass. Available in Shelf layout.

Mark favourite games with the star in a console’s game library. The TV rotates through them. Intro videos play once per selection, silently; reduced motion shows their first frame. Without your own clip, the TV plays the labelled Shelf demo.

On PS5, use the PC intro preparation tool for your own video, then place the resulting MPEG-1 intros/<console-id>.mpg in /data/console-shelf while Shelf is closed. PNG intro pictures use the same console ID. No official console intros are bundled.
