# Public PS5 captures

Owner-selected captures of isShelf v0.1.0-beta.1 on PS5 firmware 12.20, prepared for the repository on 26 October 2026 with the owner's permission.

Screenshots were decoded from the PS5's JPEG XR files, resized from 3840 × 2160 to 1920 × 1080 and encoded as WebP without capture metadata. No UI elements were retouched. The short silent recording is an excerpt converted from the PS5's HDR capture to SDR for ordinary browsers. Original media and private capture receipts remain on the owner's PC.

Manufacturer startup sequences visible inside the TV are personal overrides, not isShelf-supplied media. They are incidental to these screenshots and this short demonstration; no standalone startup clips are distributed here. Console names and marks belong to their owners. This gallery does not change the project's artwork licences or claim manufacturer affiliation.
